# MVP Scope

## Objective

Prove that verified people can find verified properties and communicate directly without a broker.

## Included

- Account creation and owner/renter role selection
- Identity-verification workflow and status
- Owner property submission with ownership evidence
- Manual verification and moderation queues
- Verified listing search and clear verification signals
- Viewing requests, in-app messaging, rental interest, reporting, and audit history

## Explicitly deferred

- Escrow and full rent-payment infrastructure
- Automated legal enforcement and legal sufficiency claims
- Nationwide land-record automation
- Advanced screening, valuation, maintenance, insurance, and property management

## Success measures

- Verified-owner, verified-renter, and verified-listing activation
- Viewing request and completion rate
- Application and successful-rental conversion
- Time to first qualified renter
- Verification completion/failure rate, fraud reports, duplicate rate, and moderation false positives
