# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Purpose

Build awareness and early trust for a verification-first, direct-to-owner rental platform in Pakistan. The initial goal is not broad national reach; it is to build enough verified local supply and renter demand to prove the direct, brokerage-free rental loop.

For product sequencing and the identity-verification dependency, see [Product strategy and timeline](Strategy-and-Timeline.md).

## Positioning

> Verified rentals. Direct from owners. No brokerage commission.

The platform should explain that it replaces the broker's trust role with verification, transparent listing signals, safe communication, and a structured rental workflow. It should not promise legal, identity, or ownership guarantees beyond what its actual verification process supports.

## Channel strategy

### Social media: awareness and community

Use social channels to reach renters and owners where they already spend time, teach the value of direct verified rentals, and build local trust.

Initial content themes:

- How to recognize fake or stale rental listings
- What owner and property verification mean—and what they do not mean
- The cost of brokerage and the direct-rental alternative
- Local rental-search tips and neighborhood content for the launch area
- Early verified-listing stories and successful rental outcomes, with consent
- Safety guidance for viewings and conversations

Social media should direct people to a platform-controlled landing page for waitlist registration, verified-owner onboarding, and later, listings.

### Website: credibility, conversion, and owned audience

The public website is the platform's durable home. It should begin as a lightweight landing site rather than a full marketing build.

Initial website functions:

- State the owner-to-renter value proposition clearly
- Capture renter and owner waitlist interest by launch city
- Explain verification, privacy, safety, and the no-brokerage model
- Provide FAQs and a support contact path
- Link to official social channels

As verified inventory and product capability grow, the website becomes the public discovery and transaction entry point: searchable listings, sign-up, help content, and app access.

## Phased plan

| Phase | Objective | Social media role | Website role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-launch | Validate demand and recruit early users in one launch area | Education, community feedback, waitlist promotion | Landing page, waitlist, trust explanation |
| Pilot | Build verified local supply and demand | Promote verified inventory and safety guidance | Owner/renter onboarding and pilot access |
| Marketplace launch | Enable discovery and direct rental workflows | Local discovery and success stories | Listings, accounts, verification and support |
| Growth | Expand density and new locations responsibly | City-specific communities and partner campaigns | Search discovery, educational content, conversion |

## Recommended initial measures

- Waitlist sign-ups by role and launch area
- Verified owner onboarding completion
- Verified properties available in the pilot area
- Renter activation and viewing requests
- Website visitor-to-waitlist conversion
- Social content reach, engagement, and referral conversion
- Successful rentals and user-reported trust

## Guardrails

- Prioritize a dense pilot geography over broad but thin national marketing.
- Do not publish raw identity data, ownership documents, exact addresses before the appropriate viewing stage, or private renter information.
- Use clear, evidence-based wording for verification claims.
- Do not frame optional platform services as hidden brokerage fees.
- Treat social channels as distribution, not the system of record for user data, support cases, listings, or trust decisions.

## Near-term decisions

1. Select the pilot city and initial property categories.
2. Select the first social channels based on the target users in that city.
3. Define the landing-page waitlist fields and consent language.
4. Establish who owns content, community responses, and escalations.
5. Define the handoff from social inquiry to verified platform onboarding.
